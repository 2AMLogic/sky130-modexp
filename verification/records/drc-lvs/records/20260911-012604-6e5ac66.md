- **Result**:
  ### DRC — still 234 violations, now individually classified

  `status: "violations"`, `violation_count: 234` (`nwell.space.1`: 194,
  `nwell.width.1`: 40) — **unchanged** from the superseded record; this
  record does not fix any violation.

  **Classification** (full per-violation detail:
  `artifacts/20260911-012604-6e5ac66/classification.json`):

  - **73 of 194 `nwell.space.1` violations — (iii) check-engine
    limitation.** Each touches exactly one merged `nwell.drawing` polygon
    (a large, non-box, 16-24+ vertex outline — confirmed directly from the
    merged `Region`, not inferred), i.e. a concave notch within a single
    physical well, not a gap between two distinct wells. `nwell.space.1`
    is transcribed as `check="space"` (`Region.space_check`), which —
    unlike the source `sky130.lydrc` rule `nwell.2a`'s `isolated` semantics
    — also flags same-polygon notches (documented in the rule's own
    docstring, `src/klayout_tools/decks/sky130.py`, "no corpus regression
    found... but that doesn't rule out a false positive specific to this
    design's own nwell geometry" — this record is exactly that check, and
    finds the false positive it left open). Filed upstream, generic, no
    design-specific detail:
    [klayout-tools#1654](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/1654).
  - **121 of 194 `nwell.space.1` violations — (ii) accepted/documented
    gap.** Each touches two or more *distinct* merged `nwell.drawing`
    polygons, cross-referenced against `layout/modexp.def` placement to
    confirm each pair belongs to two different, non-abutting standard-cell
    instances (41 within one placement row, 80 spanning a row boundary).
    This traces directly to `layout/README.md`'s already-documented "no
    tapcell insertion... no filler-cell insertion" limitation: without
    filler cells bridging the gap between sparsely-placed instances, two
    separate PMOS wells end up closer than the 1.27 um `nwell.2a` threshold
    with no filler-cell nwell material between them. Not a check-engine
    defect (the check-primitive approximation noted above applies only to
    the 73 same-polygon cases) and not a newly-discovered design defect —
    it is the same, pre-existing sparse-placement gap manifesting on a rule
    this design was never checked against before issue #78's deck-coverage
    bump.
  - **40 of 40 `nwell.width.1` violations — (ii) accepted/documented gap.**
    Every one touches exactly one merged, non-box, many-vertex polygon at a
    locally narrow "waist" formed where two (or more) misaligned adjacent
    standard-cell instances' `nwell` rectangles partially overlap or
    corner-touch. Ruled out as a library-cell or check-engine defect: every
    one of the 718 individual, per-instance, unmerged `nwell.drawing`
    rectangles measures >= 1.76 um wide x 1.605 um tall (well above the
    0.84 um threshold) — no library cell's own `nwell` geometry is
    sub-threshold; the narrow waist is purely an artifact of *merging*
    correctly-drawn, individually-correct rectangles at a placement offset
    the missing filler-cell flow never smooths over. `nwell.width.1`'s own
    docstring carries no approximation caveat (unlike `nwell.space.1`'s),
    so this is not a check-engine limitation — it is the same
    sparse-placement gap as above, on the *width* failure mode rather than
    spacing.

  **No violation in this set is classified (i) — a real design defect
  requiring an unplanned layout/floorplan change.** Every one traces to
  either an already-documented flow limitation (missing filler-cell
  insertion) or a check-engine approximation gap, both now evidenced and
  cross-linked rather than merely asserted.

  **This record intentionally does not regenerate `layout/modexp.gds`.**
  Closing the 161 documented-gap violations for real needs filler-cell (and
  ultimately tapcell/PDN) insertion in the place-and-route flow — a new
  flow capability, not a `flow/par-modexp.json` parameter tweak, and out of
  issue #79's scope per its own "Out of scope" section. Follow-up:
  [sky130-modexp#81](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sky130-modexp/issues/81).
  Closing the 73 check-engine-limitation violations needs an upstream
  `klt`/`klayout-tools` fix (an `"isolated"` check-kind primitive, or an
  equivalent). Neither closure is attempted here.
