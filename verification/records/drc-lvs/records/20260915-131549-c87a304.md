- **Result**: **Root cause identified: intentional (if confusingly
  documented) semantics, not a field mix-up.** `klt lvs`'s
  `net_correspondence[]` is a single flat table of every successful
  layout↔reference pairing the comparer made **across the whole compared
  hierarchy** — one entry per matched top-level net *and* one entry per
  matched-subcircuit-declaration local pin name (each carrying its own
  `"pin": <bool>` flag, `true` exactly when that entry is one of *its own
  circuit's* declared pins) — confirming
  `docs/cli/lvs.md`'s documented invariant `len(net_correspondence) ==
  counts.nets.matched` and its `pin` field definition ("whether this net
  is one of the compared circuit's declared pins ... read from the layout
  side") directly against the pinned engine, not by inference alone.
  `counts.nets.matched` = `len(net_correspondence)` (every entry,
  regardless of the `pin` flag); `counts.pins.matched` = the count of
  those entries with `"pin": true`. Neither is scoped to the top circuit
  only, unlike `counts.{nets,pins}.{layout,reference}`, which *are*
  top-circuit-scoped — that scope mismatch between the two sides of the
  same field is exactly what makes `matched` able to exceed `layout`/
  `reference`, and is the finding filed upstream (see "Links" below).
  Match-case run
  (`artifacts/20260915-131549-c87a304/synth_match_result.json`):
  `status: "match"`; `counts.nets = {layout: 3, reference: 3, matched: 8}`,
  `counts.pins = {layout: 2, reference: 2, matched: 7}`. `net_correspondence`
  has 8 entries total (2 top-level pins `A`/`B` + 1 top-level-internal net
  `N1`, `pin: false`, + 5 subcircuit-local pins `P1`/`P2`/`P3`/`Q1`/`Q2`,
  each `pin: true`) — `8 == counts.nets.matched` exactly, and `7` (all
  entries but `N1`) `== counts.pins.matched` exactly. Mismatch-case run
  (`artifacts/20260915-131549-c87a304/synth_mismatch_result.json`):
  `status: "mismatch"`, `mismatch_count: 2` (the top `TOP` circuit itself,
  `side: "both"`, plus `CELL3`, `side: "layout"`) — exactly the real
  `modexp` report's own "side: both" pattern. `counts.pins = {layout: 2,
  reference: 2, matched: 5}`, `counts.nets = {layout: 3, reference: 3,
  matched: 5}` — once the top circuit itself could not be matched, **no**
  top-level net/pin entries appear in `net_correspondence` at all (0 of
  `A`, `B`, `N1`); all 5 entries are the still-matched `CELL1`/`CELL2`
  subcircuits' own local pins, every one `pin: true`, so `nets.matched`
  and `pins.matched` coincide exactly — the same 333/333 coincidence the
  real `modexp` report shows, now reproduced on demand and traced to its
  exact mechanism rather than merely hypothesized. This directly confirms
  `layout/lvs/README.md`'s own prior "subcircuit-pin correspondences, not
  only the top-level I/O pins" hypothesis and rules out a genuine
  net/pins-value field swap: a swap would have made `pins.matched`
  identically equal `nets.matched` in **every** case including the
  fully-matched one, but the match-case run above shows `nets.matched` (8)
  `!= pins.matched` (7) whenever a top-level internal net actually exists
  — the two fields only coincide when, as in the mismatch case (and in
  the real `modexp` report), zero top-level net entries survive.
