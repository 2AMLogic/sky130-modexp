  - Fresh extraction: 798 nets (was 771), 61 cell types (was 59), 3074 instances,
    70 pins. The `X` cards no longer carry the `VDD VDD` tokens on the `VPB` and
    `VPWR` positions of every cell: `VPWR` still reaches `VDD`, but `VPB` is an
    anonymous per-row net.
  - **VPB investigation (what was measured).** (1) The 26 `VPB` nets are the 26
    placement-row nwells: a flat merge of the GDS `nwell` layer (64/20) yields
    exactly 26 polygons, and each contains at least one `tap` diffusion shape
    (65/44; 265 tap shapes lie inside nwell). (2) `sky130_fd_sc_hd__tapvpwrvgnd_1`
    is abstracted with ports `VGND VPWR` only -- the PDK's LEF gives it no `VPB`
    pin; its `VPWR` pin carries an `nwell` shape -- while the filler masters
    expose `VPB`. The abstracted netlist therefore contains no element that
    joins a row's `VPB` net to `VDD`; the klt-0.4.0 extraction joined them. (3)
    The signal-net portion is unaffected: the `X`-card signal nets and the
    instance bodies are the same, and the derived post-route Verilog differs
    only in renumbered anonymous, unconnected nets (see the gate-level record).
    **Not established**: whether the tap's n-tap contact physically reaches the
    `VPWR` rail in the drawn layout (that needs a flat, non-abstracted
    extraction of the tap cell, or an independent rail-continuity check), and
    therefore whether the fresh `mismatch` is an extraction-model limitation or
    a real defect. The finding is reported as measured; neither reading is
    assumed.
  - **Exploratory probe, not evidence (unpinned klt).** The same extraction
    and `klayout` gate-level LVS were repeated once with a throwaway
    `klayout-tools==0.7.0` (outside the repo pin; scratch output only, nothing
    committed). That extraction writes `VSS VDD VDD` on the cell `X` cards as
    the klt-0.4.0 one did, and its `power_connectivity` is `match` with no
    findings -- while its topology comparison reports a different residue
    (82 mismatches / 80 errors vs 13 / 12 here), which was not investigated.
    This is consistent with the shape of the already-tracked, closed upstream
    report `2AMLogic/klayout-tools#2082` (well-body pin reported inconsistent
    when abstraction discards the well geometry), but it does not prove that
    is the cause here, and it is not a reproduction at the pinned toolchain.
    No new klt issue was filed: the gap is already tracked. See decision record 0010.
  - Reproducibility: the superseded `power_connectivity: match` was a property of
