  ### Claim B — the lever matrix (supporting evidence, nothing landed)

  Seven builds, all `WIDTH=16`, all through `flow/par-modexp.json`'s
  floorplan, all characterized by `klt sta` at the same eighteen corners.
  "Closed" = `timing_status: "constrained"` with non-negative setup **and**
  hold slack.

  - **Row 1 discharges record `0002` Decision 3 step 1**: a
    timing-constrained synthesis at the binding corner, which that record
    named as the cheapest experiment and left untried, is worth
    **≈1.1 MHz of a ≈77 MHz gap** and closes **no additional corner**.
  - **The dominant, previously-unmeasured lever is standard-cell family
    selection — specifically on the falling edge.** Per-cell,
    per-transition-polarity delay *floors* (the smallest value in any
    `cell_fall`, resp. `cell_rise`, table of any arc — what no path through
    the cell can beat on that edge, whichever pin switches, regardless of
    load or slew) were extracted from the installed liberty at both corners
    for all 351 combinational cells
    (`candidates/cell-delay-floors.json`, which carries the definitions,
    both reductions, and the per-cell numbers).

    The **ratio** is not the finding: inverting cells derate 2.5–5x between
    these corners too, and more than that on their rising edge. The
    **absolute falling-edge magnitude** is. At `ss_n40C_1v28` the
    non-inverting compound family's falling floors run one to two orders of
    magnitude above the inverting family's for comparable fan-in —
    `or4_1` **4.4591 ns**, `or3_1` 3.0074, `maj3_1` 2.8753, `mux2_1`
    2.6321, `a311o_1` 2.2119, against `nand4_1` 0.1553, `nand2_1` 0.0674,
    `nor2_1` 0.0365, `inv_1` 0.0357. One `or4_1` falling arc is **45% of
    the whole 10.0 ns period** before any load or wire. At `tt_025C_1v80`
    the same pair (`or4_1` 0.3558 / `nand4_1` 0.0376) holds nearly the same
    *ratio* while both are negligible against the budget — which is exactly
    why a mapping that is clean at the nominal corner does not survive the
    corner move, and why the measured critical path propagates a falling
    edge through all eight of its non-inverting compound cells.

    **209 of the 351 combinational cells** have at least one input pin
    that, on at least one edge, cannot switch in under 1.0 ns at
    `ss_n40C_1v28` — above 10% of the whole clock period, for one gate,
    before any load. That criterion
    (`worst_arc_any_polarity_floor_ns_ss_n40C_1v28 > 1.0`, carried per-cell
    in the frozen table) **is** the exclusion list rows 2/5/6 were mapped
    with; the 209 names in `candidates/yosys-cell-exclusion.ys` reproduce
    from it exactly.
  - **Row 6 closes 18/18 by `klt sta`** — and its own in-flow
    place-and-route STA, which runs `estimate_parasitics -global_routing`
    first, reports **−1.105 ns / 90.05 MHz** at the same corner on the
    same database. Both numbers are real; they disagree about closure.
    This is recorded here **with the disagreement intact**, not resolved
    in the favourable direction.
  - Row 6's cost, stated with the result and never apart from it:
    **+522 cells** over the committed design (682 → 1204) and a latency of
    `(WIDTH + popcount(exp))*(WIDTH*(2*WIDTH+6)+2) + WIDTH + 3` cycles,
    ≈**33x** the committed core's cycle count at `WIDTH=16` (19539 cycles
    against 595 for an all-ones exponent, from the two formulas' own
    ratified/tested definitions; the ratio is 31.9-32.8x across
    `popcount(exp)` = 0..16). **At 105.14 MHz against 22.80 MHz that is a
    ~4.6x clock gain for a ~33x cycle-count increase — a net throughput
    loss of roughly 7x.** Row 6 is a *closure* result, not a throughput
    result, and must never be quoted as one.

- **Limitations of this evidence, explicitly**:
  - **No SPEF / no extracted parasitics** (`request.spef` omitted), so
    both claims' `klt sta` numbers are optimistic relative to a
    routing-estimated or extracted basis. For Claim A this understates the
    gap at the binding corner; for Claim B row 6 it is the difference
    between closing and not closing. Feeding `klt extract --parasitics`
    output in first needs the net-name-correlation problem issue #55
    evidenced.
  - **Ideal, not propagated, clock**, and an extrapolated rather than
    bisected `fmax_mhz` — documented limits of `klt sta` itself
    (`docs/cli/sta.md`).
  - **This is STA, not simulation.** Per-corner *functional* evidence is a
    separate leg (`verification/records/gate-level-sim/`).
  - **Claim B's rows 2/5/6 are off-`klt` by construction** (direct Yosys
    invocation for the exclusion list only; every other stage of every row
    is a `klt` verb). The exact script is frozen as
    `candidates/yosys-cell-exclusion.ys` so the run is reproducible by
    hand even though it is not reproducible by `klt synthesize`.
  - **Claim B's cell counts are pre-P&R** `klt synthesize`
    `instance_count`s; P&R adds tapcells, filler, CTS and repair
    instances, so they are comparable to each other and to
    `docs/baseline.md`'s 682, not to a placed-instance count.
