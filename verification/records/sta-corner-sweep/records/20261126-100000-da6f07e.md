# Record 20261126-100000-da6f07e

- **Record ID**: 20261126-100000-da6f07e
- **Claim**: issue #446 (child of #12), the single-corner probe DR-0017 requires
  before any production RTL change -- do the **two pipelined column halves**
  (DR-0014 candidate 2, two clocks per Montgomery iteration) give an
  **arithmetically correct** carry-save kernel that meets 10 ns (100 MHz) with
  nonnegative setup **and** hold slack at `ss_n40C_1v28`? Finding: **no.** The
  as-built probe P13 is correct and misses by **-5.033 ns (66.52 MHz)**; P13b,
  the same datapath and cut with the phase control replicated, is correct and
  misses by **-1.006 ns (90.86 MHz)**. Both reproduce exactly. Hold is clean
  (+2.5 ns). Nothing here changes the committed design, `layout/`, `rtl/` or
  the spec.
- **Design provenance**: `rtl/modexp.v` @ `da6f07e` is untouched. P13/P13b are
  timing-probe kernels frozen under
  `artifacts/20261126-100000-da6f07e/candidates/rtl/`, named `modexp` only so
  the committed `flow/` requests apply (probe interface, not the modexp
  handshake). Both are the radix-2 carry-save kernel of records `0011`-`0016`
  at `WIDTH=16`, `N = WIDTH+2 = 18` columns, `R = 2^N`.
  - **Cut `h`**: `H = N/2 = 9`. Low group = columns `0..8` (holds column 0, so
    produces `q`); high group = columns `9..17`. `h` was **not swept**; one cut
    was probed. Which other `h` would help is not stated here (but see the
    "worst path" finding below: the binding path is not a function of `h`).
  - **Boundary signals** (one iteration = two clocks, never overlapped).
    *Low clock* (`ph=0`): columns `0..8` advance. The only high-group value read
    is column 9's first-level sum `s1[9] = S[9]^C[9]^bi[9]` (old values; the high
    group has not moved yet). The carry leaving the top low column,
    `c1[8]`, is captured in register `cb`. *High clock* (`ph=1`): columns
    `9..17` advance using `cb` as the carry into column 9 and the quotient bit
    register `qr` as `q`. At the end of that clock `a`/`bi_r` shift.
  - **When the shifted bit returns to the low half**: the one-bit right shift
    means new column `j` takes `s2[j+1]`, so the high group's new column 9
    (written in the high clock) is what the *next* low clock reads as `S[9]`,
    `C[9]` -- one full clock later. That is the single iteration register in the
    loop (DR-0017 "Where the register sits").
  - **How `q` reaches the high half**: `q` is formed in the *high* clock of the
    previous iteration from the already-updated column 0 and
    `bi_next[0] = a[0] & b[0]`, and held in `qr` for both clocks of the current
    iteration. The low clock therefore starts from a flop, not from a
    three-input XOR of column 0.
  - P13b differs from P13 **only** in control: the single `ph` flop is replaced
    by eight phase flops that toggle from their own output (structurally
    distinct, so Yosys cannot merge them), each driving one slice of the
    enables. The datapath, cut and schedule are identical.
- **Arithmetic check** (`tools/tb_kernel_2cycle.v`, Icarus 13.0): for each
  product, run `WIDTH+2` complete low/high pairs, sample the settled `S`, `C`,
  and require `(T*R) mod m == (a*b) mod m` and `0 <= T < 2m`, `T = S + C`,
  `R = 2^(WIDTH+2)` (DR-0017's schedule). Odd moduli `m >= 3`, `a, b < m`.
  Seed 3, 2000 cases per width. **Mismatch count 0 at every verified width, for
  both kernels**:

  | WIDTH | cases | mismatches | `q=1` iterations | `q=0` iterations | `cb=1` (carry across the cut) | `cb=0` |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 4 | 2000 | 0 | 3814 | 8186 | 596 | 11404 |
  | 6 | 2000 | 0 | 6346 | 9654 | 1438 | 14562 |
  | 8 | 2000 | 0 | 8630 | 11370 | 2371 | 17629 |
  | 16 | 2000 | 0 | 17021 | 18979 | 6518 | 29482 |

  (Identical for P13 and P13b, as expected: same datapath. Counts are over
  iterations, at the sampling points of the testbench.) Both quotient-bit values
  and carries across the cut are exercised at every width. This is a kernel
  self-check; it is not the bit-exact cocotb suite and no production design
  exists.
- **Run configuration**: the harness of record `20261029-040000-cf81741`
  (`tools/eval.sh`, byte-identical; its `sha256` is `d61113f6...`): `klt
  synthesize` with `flow/synthesize-modexp.json` -> `flow/tie_constants.py` ->
  `klt place-and-route` with `flow/par-modexp.json` (tt corner, 100 MHz target,
  seed 42, `WIDTH=16`) -> `klt sta` on the **probe's own routed DEF**, period
  10.0 ns, `pdk.corners` rewritten to the single `ss_n40C_1v28`. Tools: the
  pinned `klayout-tools==0.8.0` and `yowasp-yosys==0.68.0.0.post1208` from
  `scripts/setup-env.sh` (venv in the worktree), `ciel` PDK `open_pdks f6eeac7`.
  **Single corner only; the 18-corner sweep was not run.** Each kernel was run
  **twice** with identical RTL, cut and pins. `klt` was driven throughout;
  Yosys and OpenROAD were not invoked by hand except for the post-hoc worst-path
  text (`tools/path.sh`, which reads the same routed DEF and liberty, as in
  DR-0016's record).
- **Statistical convention**: deterministic runs. Both reruns are identical in
  unrounded WNS (`-5.03264` ns for P13, `-1.00568` ns for P13b), hold slack
  (`2.50557`, `2.58464` ns) and Fmax (`66.5219`, `90.8622` MHz); the raw files
  differ only in scratch paths and invocation ids. As in earlier records the
  placer seed does not move this flow and variance across netlist perturbations
  is not characterised; differences under ~1 ns are within noise.
- **Result**: **Reproducible miss; the pipelined-column-halves probe fails its
  pass test** (setup and hold both nonnegative at 10 ns).

  | id | kernel | WNS (ns) | f_max (MHz) | hold slack (ns) | instances | arithmetic |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | P12 (record `20261029-040000-cf81741`) | single-cycle, q-loop mux2 | -4.081 | 71.02 | +2.496 | 451 | 0 errors |
  | P13 | two clocks/iteration, column halves, one shared phase flop | -5.033 | 66.52 | +2.506 | 449 | 0 errors, W=4/6/8/16 |
  | P13b | P13 with eight replicated phase flops | -1.006 | 90.86 | +2.585 | 459 | 0 errors, W=4/6/8/16 |

  `timing_status` is `constrained` for both; `constraint_completeness` is
  `incomplete` only because the 34 `s_out`/`c_out` output ports carry no output
  delay in the committed request (0 unclocked registers), the same as P12.

  - **P13's worst path is control fanout, not the datapath**
    (`results/p13-worst-path.txt`, arrival 13.36 ns, setup 1.67): phase flop
    2.22 -> `nor2_2` 4.64 (fanout 38) -> `buf_4` 4.27 (fanout 45) -> `a31oi` 0.81
    -> `a22o` 1.41 -> state flop D. A single phase flop decoded into every
    enable costs ~8.9 ns at this corner. This is an artifact of the probe's
    control, not of the cut, and is why P13b was run.
  - **P13b's worst path is a single column's depth, and the cut does not touch
    it** (`results/p13b-worst-path.txt`, arrival 9.33 ns, setup 1.67): flop 2.16
    -> `xor3_2` 3.98 (fanout 3) -> `xor2` 1.23 -> `xnor2` 0.63 -> `a22o` 1.33 ->
    D. That is the two chained 3:2 levels (`S^C^bi` then `^c1s^qm`) inside one
    column. Splitting the columns into a low and a high group puts a register
    *between* columns; it does not shorten the vertical depth of any one
    column. This is the point DR-0017 "Not claimed" flagged ("the column cut
    alone does not shorten the depth of a column") and the measurement
    confirms it: removing the control artifact leaves -1.006 ns on the datapath.
  - **Data-path-only floor is therefore ~ -1 ns at this corner for this cut and
    kernel form**, 0.76 ns better than the best earlier kernel P8 (-1.764 ns,
    85.00 MHz, single cycle) but still negative. One cut (`H = 9`) and one
    control form were probed; this is not a claim about every `h` or every
    control form.
  - Not built, and not claimed: a complete design, the cocotb suite, the cycle
    count of a real implementation, the 18-corner sweep, parasitic-basis
    comparison. No throughput or MHz claim is made for a pipelined design.
- **Disposition**:
  - **Fail on a correct kernel.** The probe is complete. DR-0017's contingent
    supersession of record `0001` Decision 3's latency paragraph is **to lapse**
    for the column-halves cut (its condition -- a pipelined-halves RTL change
    that clears the gates -- cannot be met when the probe itself misses 100 MHz
    at the binding corner). Record `0001` stays in force **unamended**. T1 item
    5 remains the disclosed FAIL (DR-0004); the 100 MHz target and the 18-corner
    matrix are unchanged. DR-0017 is **not edited** here; the standing change is
    proposed in decision record `0019` for the two-key step.
  - **Not probed (scope)**: the *level* cut (a register between the two 3:2
    levels, which DR-0017 prices at the same `K = 2`) is a different candidate
    from the column halves this issue names and was not run. Whether to spend
    another probe on it is an operator decision, not something this record
    pre-empts. No follow-on RTL issue is filed (the pass branch is not taken).
