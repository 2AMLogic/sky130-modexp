# Record 20261029-040000-cf81741

- **Record ID**: 20261029-040000-cf81741
- **Claim**: issue #271, operator ruling of 2026-10-29 (probe (b)) -- does the
  q-loop restructure of DR-0015 option 2 give an **arithmetically correct**
  carry-save Montgomery kernel that meets 10 ns (100 MHz) with **positive**
  slack at `ss_n40C_1v28`? Finding: **no.** P12 is correct (0 errors) but
  its WNS is **-4.081 ns (71.02 MHz)**, worse than P8 (-1.764 ns). Probe (b)
  fails its pass test. Nothing here changes the committed design, `layout/`
  or any T1 verdict.
- **Design provenance**: `rtl/modexp.v` @ `cf81741` is untouched. P12 is a
  new timing-probe kernel frozen at
  `artifacts/20261029-040000-cf81741/candidates/rtl/p12-q-loop-single-mux2.v`,
  named `modexp` only so the committed `flow/` requests apply (probe
  interface, not the modexp handshake). It is the P8 form with
  q' = A ^ (q & ~m1) rewritten as q' = q ? d_q1 : d_q0, where A = S1^C1^
  maj(S0,C0,bi0)^x_r is q-independent and the load operand is folded into
  d_q1/d_q0, so the q loop is one mux2. The inverted q copies use their own
  mux on inverted data selected by an inverted copy.
- **Run configuration**: the harness of record `20261028-220000-c681034`
  (`tools/eval.sh`, byte-identical): `klt synthesize` with
  `flow/synthesize-modexp.json` -> `flow/tie_constants.py` -> `klt
  place-and-route` with `flow/par-modexp.json` (tt corner, 100 MHz, seed 42,
  WIDTH=16) -> `klt sta` at the single corner `ss_n40C_1v28`, period 10.0 ns.
  Tools: pinned `klayout-tools==0.8.0` and `yowasp-yosys==0.68.0.0.post1208`
  from `scripts/setup-env.sh` (venv in the worktree), `ciel` PDK
  `open_pdks f6eeac7`. One probe, run twice. Single-corner only; the
  18-corner sweep was not run. `klt` was driven throughout; Yosys was never
  run directly.
- **Statistical convention**: deterministic runs; the rerun is bit-for-bit
  identical in WNS (-4.08067 ns both runs). As in earlier records the placer
  seed does not move this flow; variance across netlist perturbations is not
  characterised, and differences under ~1 ns are within noise. A -4.08 ns
  result is far outside that noise floor.
- **Result**: **Probe (b) fails.**

  | id | kernel | WNS (ns) | f_max (MHz) | instances | self-check |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | P8 (prior record) | six q copies, shallow q recurrence | -1.764 | 85.00 | 428 | 2000 odd moduli, W=8/16, 0 errors |
  | P12 | P8 with the q loop as a single mux2 | -4.081 | 71.02 | 451 | same, 0 errors (W=8 and W=16) |

  Hold slack +2.496 ns; `timing_status` `constrained`.

  - **Correct but not timing-positive.** Self-check: `tb_kernel_1cycle.v`,
    W=8 and W=16, 2000 odd moduli each, 0 errors (P8 control, W=16: 0 errors).
  - **The mux2 is at the end of the worst path, not the problem.** Worst path
    (`results/p12-worst-path.txt`, arrival 12.28 ns, setup 1.80): flop 2.19 ->
    `maj3` 3.06 -> `xor3` 3.49 -> `nor2` 0.60 -> `o21ai` 0.40 -> `mux2` 2.53
    -> q copy D. The `maj3`/`xor3` is the q-independent cone A (t2 and the
    S1^C1^t2^x_r XOR) which the restructure leaves in front of the select; the
    mux2 is 2.53 ns at this corner. Hoisting the q-independent part out of the
    q term did not shorten the path, it made A the path, and the explicit
    select adds a mux delay behind it. The restructure therefore did not
    "leave a single mux2 on the q loop" in a timing sense: the loop through q
    is short, but the cone that feeds the mux is longer than P8's whole path.
  - **Q fan-out.** The worst-path flop has fan-out 4; the largest input-pin
    fan-out of any non-clock/reset net in the synthesized netlist is 10
    (script count, not q-specific), as in earlier records.
  - **Endpoint slack list was not produced** for this probe (single worst
    path only); the ranking of S/C versus q endpoints is therefore not
    stated here.
  - Not built, and not claimed: complete design, cocotb suite, cycle count,
    18-corner sweep, parasitic-basis comparison.
