# Record 20261027-120000-98bf55d

- **Record ID**: 20261027-120000-98bf55d
- **Claim**: issue #234 (T1 item 5, corner verification) -- a **candidate
  study**: does any microarchitecture close `ss_n40C_1v28` at the ratified
  100 MHz without the bit-serial throughput loss? Finding: **no complete
  candidate does.** Nothing here changes the committed design, `layout/`,
  or any T1 verdict; nothing is landed.
- **Design provenance**: `rtl/modexp.v` @ `98bf55d` is the baseline; each
  candidate is a modification of it (full designs C1-C3) or a standalone
  timing-probe kernel (P1-P3), frozen under
  `artifacts/20261027-120000-98bf55d/candidates/rtl/`. See that directory's `README.md`.
- **Run configuration**: per candidate, one run of
  `artifacts/20261027-120000-98bf55d/candidates/tools/eval.sh` (`klt synthesize` with
  `flow/synthesize-modexp.json` -> `flow/tie_constants.py` ->
  `klt place-and-route` with `flow/par-modexp.json` (tt corner, 100 MHz,
  seed 42) -> `klt sta` at the single corner `ss_n40C_1v28`, period 10.0 ns).
  Pinned `.venv` from `scripts/setup-env.sh` (klt `d1dc0fc`, YoWASP Yosys
  0.68 by content, open_pdks `f6eeac7`); OpenROAD 26Q3-1510-g6cb3f2b704.
  Single-corner runs only; the 18-corner sweep was **not** run because no
  candidate reached the point where it would be informative.
- **Statistical convention**: deterministic single runs (seed 42 for P&R;
  cycle counts from a fixed-seed Icarus run). No repetition, so
  run-to-run synthesis/placement variance is **not** characterized (see
  Result, P2 vs P1).
- **Result**: **No candidate qualifies; item 5 stays `unmet`.**
  Binding corner `ss_n40C_1v28`, setup, 100 MHz target (`f_max = 1/(10 - WNS)`
  as `klt sta` reports). Cycles are the testbench's start-to-done count at
  `WIDTH=16`, all-ones exponent (DR-0007 counts one more; same convention
  inside this table). Throughput = f_max / cycles x 1000.

  | id | candidate | WNS (ns) | f_max (MHz) | cycles | thr. | instances | cocotb cross-check |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | C0 | baseline `rtl/modexp.v`, re-run under this pin | -30.332 | 24.79 | 594 | 41.7 | 717 | existing evidence |
  | C1 | speculative parallel reduction (`+` adders) | -44.941 | 18.20 | 594 | 30.6 | 787 | 2000/2000 pass, W=4,6,8,16 |
  | C2 | C1 with Kogge-Stone prefix adders | -19.536 | 33.86 | 594 | 57.0 | 1601 | 2000/2000 pass, W=4,6,8,16 |
  | C3 | step split in two cycles (register the 2p+b sum) | -17.713 | 36.08 | 1106 | 32.6 | 802 | 2000/2000 pass, W=4,6,8,16 |
  | P1 | radix-2 Montgomery, carry-save, 4:2 per cycle (kernel) | -2.448 | 80.33 | n/a | n/a | 420 | kernel self-check only |
  | P2 | P1 with quotient bit registered (kernel) | -5.491 | 64.55 | n/a | n/a | 424 | kernel self-check only |
  | P3 | P1 split into two 3:2 stages, one lane (kernel) | -3.071 | 76.51 | n/a | n/a | 644 | kernel self-check only |

  Hold slack is non-negative in every run (+2.07 ns for C0-C3; +2.4 to +2.6
  ns for P1-P3). `timing_status` is `constrained` in every run.

  - C0's -30.332 ns differs from the committed layout's -33.867 ns
    (`20261015-205405-4d8d397`) because this is a **fresh** synth + P&R under
    the current pin (717 instances, not the committed layout's 682-cell
    netlist); it is the like-for-like reference for C1-C3, not a
    replacement for the committed number.
  - C1-C3 are the architectures a "shorten `S_MM_RUN`" search suggests.
    None closes. The flow maps `+` to a ripple chain (C0's worst path is
    a `maj3` carry chain through 19 cells, clk-to-Q 3.5 ns, setup 1.7 ns),
    so restructuring the compare/subtract order (C1) is slower; C2's prefix
    adders buy +37% throughput at 2.2x the instances and still miss by
    19.5 ns. C3 costs 1.86x the cycles for a 1.46x clock: net throughput
    below baseline.
  - P1-P3 are **timing probes of a Montgomery datapath, not designs**: they
    have no exponentiation control, no Montgomery-domain conversion (R^2 mod
    m), no carry-save-to-binary conversion and no final reduction, and no
    modexp cocotb suite exists for them. Each was checked only against its own
    arithmetic (`tb_kernel_*.v`: 2000 random odd moduli at W=8 and W=16,
    (S+C)*2^W = a*b mod m, S+C < 3m, 0 errors). They are reported because
    they bound what a Montgomery candidate could reach, not as candidates.
    The best (P1) misses by 2.45 ns; P2's registered-q variant is *worse*
    (the 31-fanout flop was left unbuffered), and P3's worst path is the
    ~115-fanout `ph`/`load` enable network (8.3 ns elapsed before the first
    datapath gate), not the 3:2 layer. Placement/buffering variance dominates
    differences of this size; single runs cannot rank P1/P2/P3 firmly.
  - Overhead floor at this corner: clock-to-Q (1.8-3.8 ns) plus setup
    (0.6-1.9 ns) is 2.4-5.7 ns of the 10 ns budget, and one `xnor3` is
    3.3-3.6 ns, so a closing datapath has roughly two gate levels. Only a
    carry-save form with no carry-propagate or comparison per cycle is
    anywhere near; every candidate that keeps one (C0-C3) is 17.7-44.9 ns short.
  - **Throughput bar** (issue definition: a candidate may spend cycles up
    to the ratio of its clock to the baseline's): against C0 a 100 MHz
    design may use up to 100/24.79 = 4.03x the cycles. A complete
    Montgomery design's cycle count was **not measured** (no such design was
    built) and is therefore not claimed.
