  `request.power`: tapcell + PDN + filler-cell insertion), which changes
  the superseded record's own cited `layout/modexp.gds` content hash from
  `sha256:229ed6a5...` to `sha256:9fa0dbe1...` above — `verification/
  check_records.py`'s freshness check would otherwise flag the superseded
  record (still "live," i.e. not previously superseded) as stale the
  moment `layout/modexp.gds` changed, even though nothing about this
  experiment's own method or verdict was touched. This record exists
  solely to re-pin the chain's "live" pointer forward, exactly as
  `verification/records/place-and-route/records/20260816-072918-d8eafca.md`
  did for an unrelated reason in that experiment.
  **The superseded record's Leg 1 PASS verdict is not re-verified here**
  and its own findings remain valid history against the pre-#81, no-PDN
  layout it was actually measured against. **Attempting the equivalent
  re-derivation against the new, PDN-equipped `layout/modexp.gds` was
  tried and found blocked**: `klt extract --abstract-cells` against the
  new GDS now reports `VDD`/`VSS` as genuine top-level pins (correctly —
  a real PDN gives this design, for the first time, a continuous, labeled
  power net reaching the die boundary), and
  `verification/gate-level/spice_to_verilog.py`'s netlist-derivation
  validator — written against a no-PDN layout with no such pins — rejects
  this as three validation problems (`net VDD is used as both a power pin
  and a signal pin`, `top-level pin VSS is connected to no cell pin`, `15
  net(s) ... absent from the extract report's nets[]`). This is a gap in
  this repo's own `verification/gate-level/spice_to_verilog.py`, not a
  `klt`/klayout-tools defect, and updating it is out of issue #81's own
  DRC/LVS-focused scope — filed as a dedicated follow-up:
  [sky130-modexp#83](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sky130-modexp/issues/83).
  `layout/lvs/modexp_layout_abstracted.spice` and
  `layout/lvs/modexp_layout_extract_report.json` (this experiment's
  upstream inputs, per `verification/gate-level/run-gate-level-sim.sh`)
  are **deliberately left unchanged**, still describing the pre-#81 GDS —
  updating them was tried and reverted for the same reason (see issue #83
  for the attempted derivation and its validation errors); regenerating
  them is that issue's own job, once `spice_to_verilog.py` can model a
  PDN-equipped layout's power ports correctly.
